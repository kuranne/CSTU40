## Software Engineers by IEEE 

"The application of a **systematic**, **disciplined**, **quantifiable** approach to the development."
**disciplined** meaning it have standard, process and best practice.

> Do as Engineer, not a Craftsman.

**Quantify Projects** must:
1. Complete in planed time.
2. Everyone happy.

**The three group of Engineering:** 1. Development (Building), 2. Management (Implementing, Quality Focus), 3. Support (Similar to IT Support for Project)
