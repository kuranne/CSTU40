## Factor to changing the software

1. Every 3 years, technology will change
2. Lawsuit
3. Business Wind

Software must divide to each dev and maintain for Dev / Maintain, with **70 / 30**
## Development Cost

- **50%** — Test, QA
- **16.67%** — Coding (must not exceed 20% of Project)
- **33.33%** — Design, Analytic

## Maintain Cost

- **Corrective (20%–25%)** — Fixing unexpected bugs, errors, and security vulnerabilities after launch.
- **Adaptive (15%–20%)** — Updating software to match new operating systems, hardware, APIs, or regulations.
- **Perfective (25%–30%)** — Adding minor features, improving speed, and refining user experience based on feedback.
- **Preventive (10%–15%)** — Rewriting old code (refactoring) and updating documentation to stop future crashes
