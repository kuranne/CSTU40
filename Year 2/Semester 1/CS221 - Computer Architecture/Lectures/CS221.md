## 📚 Lecture Notes

| Week | Topic | Link | Key Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Week 1** | Concurrency, Parallelism & Hardware | [[Concurrency of CPU]] | TLP, ILP (5-stage Pipeline), SIMD, Multi-core, Hyperthreading, Von Neumann vs. Harvard |
| **Week 1 - 2** | Instruction Set Architecture (ISA) | [[Instructions]] | Machine code, Accumulator, Stack (0-addr), GPRs, Instruction Tracing |
| **Week 2** | Register Architectures & ISA Models | [[Register Architectures]] | Register-Register (Load-Store / RISC) vs. Register-Memory (CISC), Expression conversion exercises |
| **Week 3** | General Purpose Registers & Integer Logic | [[General Purpose]] | Two's Complement arithmetic, x86-64 registers (16 GPRs), AT&T vs. Intel syntax, Addressing Modes |
| **Week 4** | Assembly Compilation Pipeline | [[Assembly]] | Spec (x86_64, ELF64), Assembler $	o$ Linker $	o$ Executable flow |
| **Week 4** | Bitwise & Shift Exercises | [[Exercise]] | Step-by-step tracing for `not`, `or`, `shl`, and `shr` operations on `%rax` / `%rcx` |
