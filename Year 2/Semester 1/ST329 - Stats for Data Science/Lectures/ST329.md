# ST329 — Stats for Data Science

> **Related Course:** [[CS240]] (Data Science Applications & Modeling)

---

## Instructor Information & Course Roadmap
- **Instructor:** Monthira Duangsaphon (อ.มณฑิรา ดวงสระพล)
- **Course Description:** Rigorous probability theory, multivariate distributions, Markov models, Bayesian inference, and statistical regression modeling for data science.

> [!note] **Exam Milestones:**
> - **Midterm Exam:** Probability Theory, Random Variables, Distributions, Bayesian Inference
> - **Final Exam:** Statistical Regression Models & Applications

---

## Lecture Notes

| Week | Topic | Link | Key Highlights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Reference** | Statistical & Probability Formulae | [[Formulae]] | Probability axioms, Conditional probability, Total Probability Theorem, Bayes' Rule |
| **Week 1 - 2** | Random Variables & Distributions | [[Random Variables]] | Discrete vs. Continuous RVs, PMF/PDF, Cumulative Distribution (CDF), Expected value $E(X)$, Variance $\operatorname{Var}(X)$ |
| **Week 2 - 3** | Cumulative Distribution & Survival Function | [[Distribution]] | Cumulative Distribution Function (CDF) for discrete/continuous RVs, Survival Function $S(x) = 1 - F(x)$, mathematical properties & limits |
