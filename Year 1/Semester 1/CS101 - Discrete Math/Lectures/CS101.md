## 📌 Course Overview
Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science, serving as the foundational theoretical framework for data structures, algorithms, formal languages, databases, and theory of computation. Topics include propositional and predicate logic, proof techniques, set theory, relations, functions, combinatorics, and introductory graph theory.

## 📑 Core Topics
1. **Propositional & Predicate Logic:** Propositions, truth tables, tautologies, logical equivalences, quantifiers ($\forall, \exists$).
2. **Methods of Proof:** Direct proof, proof by contraposition, proof by contradiction, mathematical induction, and structural induction.
3. **Sets, Relations & Functions:** Set operations, Cartesian products, equivalence relations, partial orders, injective/surjective/bijective functions.
4. **Combinatorics & Counting Principles:** Multiplication and addition rules, permutations $P(n,r)$, combinations $C(n,r)$, pigeonhole principle, binomial coefficients.
5. **Graph Theory & Trees:** Directed/undirected graphs, paths, cycles, Euler and Hamiltonian circuits, trees, and minimum spanning trees.
