# Neoliberalism

**Neoliberalism** is a political and economic ideology emphasizing free markets, deregulation, and a reduced role of the state.

## Impact on the Left
According to [[Göran Therborn]]'s analysis, the rise of Neoliberalism in the late 20th century weakened the traditional Left, shifting the focus away from the welfare state and labor rights that defined the "Golden Age" of the 20th century.
