# Aging Society

An **Aging Society** refers to a demographic shift where a significant percentage of the population is elderly. 

## Standards
- **UN/WHO Standard**:
  - Aging: >= 7% of people 65+
  - Aged: >= 14% of people 65+
  - Super-Aged: >= 20% of people 65+
- **Thailand Standard**:
  - Aging: >= 10% of people 60+
  - Aged/Super-Aged: >= 20% of people 60+

## The Case of Thailand
Thailand is currently a complete Aged Society and is projected to become a Super-Aged Society by 2035. Unlike developed nations, Thailand faces the "Old Before Rich" situation, lacking sufficient post-retirement property for many.

### Contributing Factors
- Lower Total Fertility Rate (TFR) of 1.3 (normal replacement is 2.1).
- Increased longevity.
- Family structure shift from Extended to Nuclear families, often resulting in Skipped-Generation Households.
