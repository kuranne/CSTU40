# Middle Income Trap

The **Middle Income Trap** describes an economic situation where a country attains a certain average income (due to given advantages) but gets stuck at that level, unable to transition to a high-income economy.

## Thailand's Context
Thailand faces this trap because higher wages have caused foreign direct investment (FDI) to seek cheaper alternatives. The country's economy has historically been FDI-led with little homegrown innovation. To escape the trap, Thailand must shift its focus from "cheap wage" labor to "innovation and digital skills".
