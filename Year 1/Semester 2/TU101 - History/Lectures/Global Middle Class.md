# Global Middle Class

The **Global Middle Class** represents the middle-income segment of the worldwide population. 

## Dreams and Nightmares
Sociologist [[Göran Therborn]] describes a contradiction in the modern middle class:
- **The Dream**: Institutions like the World Bank see a growing middle class as a symbol of success and consumption.
- **The Nightmare**: There is a phenomenon of the "Vanishing Middle Class" in developed countries, leading to downward mobility.

## Characteristics
The modern middle class faces "precarity" (uncertainty) and a "fear of falling", often burdened by debt and increased competition from AI or corporate downsizing. While they tend to be cosmopolitan, perceived threats to their security can drive them toward far-right or nationalist ideologies.
