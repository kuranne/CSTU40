# Financialization

**Financialization** is a phenomenon where the economic system becomes dominated by the financial sector rather than the real production sector.

This concept is often discussed in relation to modern economic inequality and the precarity of the [[Global Middle Class]]. It emphasizes profit over middle-class professionalism and stability.
