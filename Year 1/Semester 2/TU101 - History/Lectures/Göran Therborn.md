# Göran Therborn

**Göran Therborn** is a renowned sociologist known for his critiques and analyses of the global class structure and political movements.

## Key Theories
- **The World and the Left**: Analyzes the decline of the Left from its 20th-century "Golden Age" to its struggles against [[Neoliberalism]] and financial capitalism.
- **Global Middle Class**: Highlights the contradiction between the "dream" of middle-class consumption and the "nightmare" of the vanishing middle class, emphasizing modern precarity and the shift towards [[Financialization]].
