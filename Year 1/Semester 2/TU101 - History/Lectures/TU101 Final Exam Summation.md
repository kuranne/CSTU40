# TU101 Final Exam Summation

This lecture summary covers material from weeks 10 to 15 of TU101, focusing on major societal and economic transitions.

## Key Themes
- **Aging Society**: Thailand is rapidly aging ("Old Before Rich"), facing a higher dependency ratio and a shift from extended to nuclear families.
- **Economic Transition**: Thailand has gone through 9 economic eras, currently facing a [[Middle Income Trap]] due to reliance on cheap labor and FDI instead of innovation.
- **The World and the Left**: The decline of the 20th-century Left in the face of [[Neoliberalism]] and the challenge of the climate crisis.
- **Global Middle Class**: Based on the theories of [[Göran Therborn]], the modern middle class faces "precarity" and "financialization", a sharp contrast to the stable "Good Job" ideal of the past.
